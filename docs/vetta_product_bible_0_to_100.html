2. The End-to-End Autonomous Product Pipeline

From initial job requisition intake to day-1 joining verification, Vetta coordinates specialized, autonomous agent swarms operating continuously across communication transports.

Step 1: Autonomous Intake & Taste Extraction (0–5 Minutes)

The hiring manager submits a role or speaks to the AI Intake Agent. The agent extracts hard constraints (CTC ceiling, notice limit, tech stack) and initializes the Client Taste Centroid in 32-dimensional latent embedding space, seeded from company culture, past hires, and engineering bar.

Step 2: Omnichannel Sourcing & Transparent Proof-of-Work Outreach (Minute 5–60)

The Sourcing Agent evaluates inbound applicants and searches passive talent networks. Outreach is dispatched via WhatsApp (primary transport), with automatic fallback to SMS (at 6 hours) and verified corporate email. Outreach messages feature explicit company attribution (*"heading talent acquisition for NexusCore"*) and 1-click DPDP opt-out buttons.

Step 3: Hinglish Phone Voice Screening & Anti-Cheating Telemetry (Hour 1–3)

Candidates complete an autonomous 12-minute phone screening. The Voice Agent handles Indian accents, code-mixed Hinglish (*"5 years ka production experience hai"*), and probes technical depth. Simultaneously, the audio stream is inspected for latency jitter (detecting proxy earpieces) and teleprompter speech entropy (detecting LLM-generated verbal answers).

Step 4: Forensic Byte-Level Verification & Dual-Threshold Ranking (Hour 3–4)

Resumes undergo byte-level PDF AST parsing, detecting Canva payslip templates, fake shell IT company domains, and UAN timeline overlaps. The Ranking Engine scores candidates using both semantic cosine similarity and the client's historical feedback centroid.

Step 5: Anonymized Blind Dossier Delivery & 1-Click Unlock

The founder receives a zero-login magic slate link. To prevent resume theft, candidates appear as Anonymized Blind Dossiers (e.g. Candidate A-101 · Senior Go Engineer · Tier-1 Payments Unicorn). The founder listens to a 45-second Hinglish technical audio snippet and inspects code proofs. They unlock full contact details, unedited resume, and calendar booking via a 1-click ₹2,499 unlock (or 8% contingency placement). Backed by our 100% Interview-Show Guarantee: credits refund instantly if a candidate fails to attend.

Step 6: Offer Guard Pre-Boarding Engagement & Silver Medalist Vault (Days 1–90)

Once an offer is accepted, Offer Guard monitors the candidate over their 60–90 day notice period with sentiment check-ins and counter-offer hazard tracking. Simultaneously, the pre-screened #2 and #3 runner-up candidates enter the Silver Medalist Talent Vault (with DPDP consent) to be routed instantly to other active client searches with zero new sourcing overhead.

Key Architecture Differentiator: Zero-Login Taste Memory Centroid

Clients never need to create an account, password, or install software. Every interaction on the shared magic link (`/share/:token`) maps to the client's organization. Every reaction ([Request Interview] = $+1.0$, [Pass with Reason] = $-0.6$) continuously recalibrates the client's 32-dimensional latent preference centroid in real time. By week 3, Vetta candidate slates reach 94%+ hiring manager interview acceptance rates.

6. Algorithmic Architecture & Latent Memory Engine

Vetta is built on high-performance vector retrieval, streaming audio models, and continuous Bayesian latent preference updates.

1. Client Taste Memory Centroid

Every hiring manager's preferences are modeled as a point in a 32-dimensional continuous latent space. When an intake occurs, the centroid $\vec{C}_0$ is initialized from role requirements.

Upon candidate review, the centroid updates dynamically:

Interview Request: C_{t+1} = Normalize(C_t + 1.0 * v_{candidate})
Pass / Rejection: C_{t+1} = Normalize(C_t - 0.6 * v_{candidate})

Final candidate ranking combines semantic role fit (weight 0.45) with taste centroid cosine alignment (weight 0.55).

2. Dual-Transport Dispatcher

Candidates are engaged across redundant, intelligent transport cascades:

  • WhatsApp Cloud API: Primary channel. Response rate 68% within 20 mins.
